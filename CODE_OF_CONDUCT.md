# Code of Conduct

Theogony is built on the conviction that knowledge infrastructure should serve humanity in all its diversity. This community is expected to reflect that conviction.

## Our Standards

We expect all contributors and participants to:

- Be respectful of different backgrounds, perspectives, and experience levels
- Accept constructive criticism gracefully
- Focus on what is best for the project and the people it serves
- Show empathy toward others in the community

We do not tolerate:

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination in any form
- Personal attacks
- Trolling or deliberately disruptive behavior
- Publishing others' private information without consent

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces: GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other community channels associated with Theogony.

## Enforcement

Violations may be reported by opening a private issue or contacting the maintainers. All reports will be reviewed and investigated. Maintainers are obligated to maintain confidentiality with regard to the reporter.
